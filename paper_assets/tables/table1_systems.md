**Table 1.** The two synthetic Texas test fleets. Capacities are comparable; Texas2k carries four times as many decision variables.

| | TX-123BT | Texas2k Series25 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Committable units | 138 | 568 |
| Committable capacity | 76.4 GW | 77.8 GW |
| Max. peak net load | 77.2 GW | 76.8 GW |
| Training / evaluation sets | 2,500 / 2,000 | 2,500 / 2,000 |

Committable capacity is the sum of `p_max_mw` over the generator portfolio. The maximum peak net load is the largest hourly net load over all 7,500 scenarios of the training and test files. The evaluation set is the first 2,000 scenarios of the test file.
